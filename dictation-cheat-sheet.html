Mac Dictation Cheat Sheet

Two features, not one. Dictation (Fn Fn) types what you say and knows punctuation and layout words. Voice Control (Accessibility) adds select, delete, move and correct by voice. Dictation settings: System Settings › Keyboard › Dictation. Voice Control settings: System Settings › Accessibility › Voice Control. Spoken on macOS 15.6 on 2026-10-05: punctuation and line breaks work; most symbols, emoji and plain-Dictation voice edits do not. Known problems

  • Tested on macOS 15.6: “new line” and “new paragraph” work. The forum reports of them inserting only a space cover 15.3.1, 15.4.1 and 26, so treat them as version-specific.
  • Tested on macOS 15.6 with the built-in mic and a Grado GW100: currency, legal and comparison signs, emoji, “no space on”, “all caps on” and the edit commands were typed as plain words.
  • Voice Control is the reliable route for editing. Select, delete, move, replace, insert, correct, undo and its modes all passed on macOS 15.6, where the plain Dictation edit commands failed.
  • Brackets, braces and “tab key” are unreliable. Closing marks work more often than opening marks.
  • Monterey (macOS 12): Apple Community reports new line did not work and pressing Return ends dictation. Workaround there: press Return, then start dictation again.
  • The MacMost sheet is v2.0 from 2019, and its Voice Control commands still passed on macOS 15.6. Say “Show commands” to see what your macOS accepts.
